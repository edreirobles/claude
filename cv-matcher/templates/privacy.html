Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

1. What we collect

When you use CV Matcher, we collect the following information:

  • Account data: your email address, used to authenticate you via magic link or Google Sign-In.
  • CV content: the CV file you upload (PDF or Word). We use it only to generate your adapted CV and delete the original file after processing.
  • Job descriptions: the job posting URL or text you provide. We store a preview (up to 2,000 characters) in your history.
  • Generated CVs: the adapted PDF we produce is stored so you can download it later.
  • Payment data: we do not store card numbers. Payments are processed entirely by Stripe.

2. How we use your data

  • To generate your tailored CV using Claude AI (Anthropic).
  • To display your generation history inside the app.
  • To manage your subscription and credits via Stripe.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or train AI models with it.

3. Third-party services

CV Matcher uses the following third parties to operate:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — processes your CV and job description to generate the adapted output. Data is sent to Anthropic's API and is subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — stores your account, generation history, and generated PDFs. Data is hosted on Supabase infrastructure. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe — handles all payment processing. We never see or store your card details. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.

4. Data retention

  • Your uploaded CV file is deleted from our servers immediately after the adapted CV is generated.
  • Generated PDFs and generation history are kept as long as your account is active.
  • You can request deletion of all your data at any time (see section 6).

5. Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for authentication (session tokens managed by Supabase). We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Export your generation history.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact us at: privacy@cvmatcher.app
